From one buyer question to the next market action. 从一个市场问题，到团队下一步该做什么。

One record moves through five acts of work. It never becomes five disconnected modules. 同一条记录依次完成五项工作，而不是被拆成五个彼此割裂的模块。

  1. 01

    Record the question 记录客户问题

    Start with the question—and the market, audience, language, and alternatives around it. 先把问题，以及对应的市场、人群、语言和比较对象记录下来。

  2. 02

    Observe the answers 观察当前答案

    See what selected AI, search, editorial, and company-owned channels are telling buyers now. 看清选定的 AI、搜索、行业内容和品牌公开信息正在怎样回答客户。

  3. 03

    Trace the reasons 追溯比较理由

    Trace each reason to supporting evidence, missing context, or contradictory information. 把每个比较理由追溯到支持它的证据、缺失的语境或相互矛盾的信息。

  4. 04

    Review an action 审阅下一步行动

    Turn the clearest gap into a prioritised action for the team to approve, adjust, or reject. 把最清楚的缺口变成一项有优先级的行动，由团队批准、调整或放弃。

  5. 05

    Ask again 再次提出问题

    Review the same question under recorded conditions. Keep changed, unchanged, and non-attributable findings separate. 在已记录条件中复核同一个问题，把已变化、未变化和无法归因的结果分开。

Representative observation—not a customer performance claim. 代表性观测，不构成客户效果声明。

One company fact. Different market conditions. 同一个公司事实，不同的市场条件。

Market, language, category terms, alternatives, sources, and observation conditions stay attached to every buyer question. 每个客户问题都保留市场、语言、当地品类用语、比较对象、来源和观测条件。全球化不是简单翻译，而是在每个市场保留正确的判断背景。

    Northstar was recommended for documented local-market support. Meridian was mentioned without the same evidence attached to the reason. Northstar 因有本地市场支持的公开记录而被推荐；Meridian 虽被提到，却没有把同类证据关联到选择理由。

  3. Reviewed action 经团队审阅的行动

    The team clarified the capability’s scope and supported markets, then attached a source a buyer could inspect. 团队明确了能力的适用范围和支持市场，并关联一份买方可以核对的公开来源。

    The reason for considering Meridian became visible. The recommendation order did not change. 考虑 Meridian 的理由已经可见，但推荐顺序没有变化。

Make the first comparison visible 让第一轮比较可见

Keep facts and conditions together 同时保留事实与条件

A company fact can stay stable while market, language, category terms, alternatives, and evidence conditions change. Both remain visible. 公司事实可以不变，市场、语言、品类用语、比较对象与证据条件却会变化。Yonaris 同时保留两者。

Keep judgement with people 把判断留给人

Yonaris can surface an observed answer, evidence, gaps, and a possible action. People decide what is accurate, appropriate, and approved—and what happens next. Yonaris 可以呈现答案、证据、缺口和可能的下一步；事实是否准确、行动是否合适、是否批准执行，仍由团队判断。

The trust boundary 信任边界

Yonaris does not promise exhaustive coverage, guaranteed ranking, guaranteed citation, autonomous execution, causal proof, or a commercial result from a single change. Yonaris 不承诺覆盖所有答案，也不保证排名、引用、自动执行、因果证明或单次变化带来的商业结果。
