# Yonaris Website 1.0 — Bilingual Copy Deck

**Status:** Draft for content review

**Date:** 2026-08-30

**Scope:** English and Simplified Chinese public website 1.0

## 1. Writing rule

The two editions share one product truth, but they are separately authored.

- English leads with a clear buyer proposition, then proves the mechanism.
- Chinese leads with a recognisable business problem, then shows what the team can do next.
- The Chinese edition is not a sentence-by-sentence translation of the English edition.
- Copy establishes the proposition. Motion explains the mechanism. Interaction lets the visitor inspect the evidence.
- Product theatre uses short labels and visible state changes instead of explanatory paragraphs.
- No edition claims exhaustive coverage, autonomous execution, guaranteed improvement, causal proof, or customer outcomes that have not been approved.

Formal category:

- **EN:** AI-Native MarTech Infrastructure
- **ZH:** AI 原生营销科技基础设施

## 5. Casework `/casework` and `/zh/casework`

### 5.1 Hero

**Eyebrow**

- **EN:** Representative casework
- **ZH:** 代表性案例演示

**Headline**

- **EN:** See one buyer question worked all the way through.
- **ZH:** 看一次完整拆解：为什么竞品先进入了备选？

**Body**

- **EN:** One representative record, from the first observation to the reviewed action, the later review, and the limits that remain.
- **ZH:** 从最初答案、证据缺口到团队行动和后续复核，一次看清判断是怎么形成的，也看清哪些结论仍然不能下。

**Disclosure**

- **EN:** Representative casework — not a customer performance claim.
- **ZH:** 代表性案例演示，不构成客户效果声明。

### 5.2 Walkthrough

`Your company` / `你的公司`, `Alternative A` / `竞品 A`, and `Alternative B` / `竞品 B` are representative labels, not real customers or competitors.

#### 1. The situation / 发生了什么

- **EN:** Your company appeared in some observations, but the answer gave buyers no clear reason to advance it into the next comparison.
- **ZH:** 营销团队发现，你的公司在选定的 AI 与数字渠道中偶尔被提及，却很少带着明确理由进入下一轮比较。

#### 2. The buyer question / 先把问题说清楚

- **EN:** Which analytics partner can support an enterprise marketing team across several markets without losing local context or evidence?
- **ZH:** 为什么竞品先进入了客户的备选，而我们的优势没有成为选择理由？

#### 3. What buyers were being told / 潜在客户当时看到了什么

- **EN:** Alternative A was recommended for documented local-market support. Your company was mentioned, but without the same evidence attached to the reason.
- **ZH:** 竞品因清楚说明适用场景并附有公开依据而进入备选；你的公司虽被提到，却只有宽泛介绍。

#### 4. What shaped the answer / 答案为什么会这样

- **EN:** Alternative A connected its capability, market conditions and public evidence. Your company published the capability, but not the conditions that made it relevant to this question.
- **ZH:** 竞品的能力、适用场景和公开依据连在一起；我们的优势缺少条件、范围和可核对来源。

#### 5. What was missing or misleading / 真正的缺口

- **EN:** The capability existed. The missing piece was a public evidence relationship that connected it to the buyer’s selection criterion.
- **ZH:** 问题不是没被提到，而是客户找不到继续比较我们的理由。

#### 6. What the team changed or validated / 团队先改什么

- **EN:** The team reviewed one evidence relationship, clarified its scope and supported markets, and attached a source that a buyer could inspect.
- **ZH:** 团队审阅并补齐一项关键证据关系：明确适用范围和支持市场，并关联潜在客户可以核对的公开来源。

#### 7. What was observed afterwards / 复核后变了什么

- **EN:** The reason for considering the company became visible in the later observation. The recommendation order did not change.
- **ZH:** 复核时，品牌的选择理由已经出现；但推荐顺序没有变化。

#### 8. What still could not be claimed / 还有什么不能下结论

- **EN:** One review cannot prove that the content change caused the answer change, or that either change produced a commercial outcome.
- **ZH:** 单次复核不能证明变化由某一项内容造成，也不能据此声称已经带来商业结果。

### 6.3 Who it is for / 谁会用 Yonaris

- **EN:** Marketing and commercial teams use Yonaris when a consequential buyer question is answered inconsistently, related evidence is unclear, or the next marketing action is difficult to prioritise.
- **ZH:** 当潜在客户得到的答案不一致、相关证据不清楚，或下一步营销行动难以排序时，Yonaris 帮助营销与商业团队看清问题。

### 6.4 Across markets / 面向多个市场

- **EN:** The company fact can stay stable while market, language, category terms, alternatives and evidence conditions change. Yonaris keeps both visible.
- **ZH:** 公司事实可以不变，市场、语言、品类用语、比较对象与证据条件却会变化。Yonaris 同时保留事实与条件，而不是把全球化等同于翻译。

### 6.5 Human judgement / 人的判断仍然重要

- **EN:** Yonaris can surface an observed answer, related evidence and gaps, and a possible next action for review. People decide what is accurate, appropriate and approved, and what the business does next.
- **ZH:** Yonaris 可以呈现观测到的答案、相关证据与缺口，以及供团队审阅的下一步。事实是否准确、行动是否合适、是否批准执行，仍由团队判断。

### 6.6 What Yonaris does not promise / 我们不承诺什么

- **EN:** Yonaris does not promise exhaustive coverage, guaranteed ranking, guaranteed citation, autonomous execution, causal proof, or a commercial result from a single change.
- **ZH:** Yonaris 不承诺覆盖所有答案，也不保证排名、引用或商业结果；系统不会绕过团队自动执行，单次变化也不能被当作因果证明。
